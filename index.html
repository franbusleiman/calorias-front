The gap in nutrition apps

AI-powered nutrition tracking already exists. Nutritionists were left out of it.

Current situation

Most nutrition-tracking apps in Latin America already use AI to read a photo and estimate calories and macronutrients. But they were built as consumer products: the professional has no access to that data, no tools to review it, and no role in how the plan gets adjusted.

calorIAs' approach

calorIAs brings the same AI capabilities to the professional side. Nutritionists get AI-assisted tools to track patients, spot patterns, and adjust plans — technology built around their workflow, not around a generic consumer app.

How it works

AI on both sides, one shared history

The tools look different depending on who's using them, but every entry feeds the same patient record.

For nutritionists

AI-powered patient tracking

  1. Every logged meal arrives organized and ready to review — not raw data to sort through by hand.

  2. Adherence trends, recurring gaps, and deviations are surfaced automatically before the consultation.

  3. Plans are adjusted using real intake data, not what the patient remembers reporting.

For patients

AI-powered meal logging

  1. Meals are logged with a single photo; AI estimates calories and macronutrients instantly.

  2. Every entry reaches the nutritionist automatically — no manual reporting required.

  3. Additional AI logging tools are being added on top of photo-based tracking.
